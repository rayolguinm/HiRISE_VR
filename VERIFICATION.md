- Twinmotion VR facts: all match the Twinmotion VR User Manual, except one file name (see corrections).

- 40-DTM explorer: post spacing, elevation range and stereo pair match the PDS labels for all 40; 36 of 40 locations matched.
- References: all 52 are real publications with matching authors, titles and journals.
- Glossary: definitions checked against the cited papers; the HiRISE, swath, focal plane, matching precision, horizontal resolution, vertical precision, ISIS and SOCET SET, NGATE, bundle adjustment, orthoimage and change-detection statements are supported.

## Functional test (19 November 2026)

Both pages were loaded in Chromium at 1440 × 900 (desktop), 402 × 874 (iPhone 18), 440 × 956 (iPhone 18 Pro Max) and 874 × 402 (iPhone landscape), and scrolled top to bottom. No page errors, no console errors, no missing files, no broken images, no dead in-page or cross-page links, no horizontal overflow; all 47 linked files exist.

Every tool was then operated, at desktop and iPhone size, and passed:

- **Home page:** cover terrain and title; Contents (7 chapters, 10 appendices); the overview stepper (4 views, 3D view, downsampling readout); the RUN.bat simulation run to PIPELINE COMPLETE; the emission-angle slider; the site folder's three states; all four flights drawing terrain; the VR speed selector; the 40-DTM explorer (Next, region filter, card loads 3D, 40 cards); the closing flight; search.
- **Reference page:** appendices A to J; ortho calculator (emission angles and Δz); triangle budget (above at factor 1, within at AUTO, 3 and 8); RUN.bat screenshots; RUN.bat simulation; troubleshooting filter; glossary (35 terms, text and category filters); 52 references, 51 with DOI links (Kirk et al. 2007 is a conference paper with no DOI); glossary links to existing references; search.

Fixed during the test: the reference page's "Skip to the content" link pointed to a removed section.

The published preview (libraries from jsDelivr) was tested the same way. Two problems there, which did not occur in the repo copy with local libraries, were found and fixed:

- **Cover shader failed to compile.** jsDelivr's `three.webgpu.min.js` imports `./three.tsl.js`, while the page mapped `three/tsl` to `three.tsl.min.js`. The page therefore loaded two copies of the TSL node library, and the cover material was compiled incorrectly. The import map now points `three/tsl` at `build/three.tsl.js`.
- **`lenis.css` not found.** `site.css` imports it from `assets/vendor`, which is not uploaded to the preview. The preview's copy now imports it from jsDelivr.

`tools/make_preview.sh` now builds the preview with these substitutions every time.
